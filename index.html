How to Play

  • It's an obby! Run, jump, double jump and dash through an obstacle course all the way to the glowing finish portal.
  • No health bar. Touch spikes, lava, fire or any monster and you die, then pop right back at your last checkpoint. Your deaths are counted in the top-right.
  • Checkpoints are numbered flags. Touch one and it turns green. That's where you come back after you die.
  • Beat your time. Your time for the level and your best time are shown in the top-right corner.
  • Mini monsters roam the course. Touching one kills you! Jump over them, or slash them for shards. Down-slash them (or spikes) to bounce!
  • Signs (the ? posts) explain each obstacle and attack. Walk up to one to read it.
  • Shards are glowing crystals to collect all over each level. Small ones are worth 1, rare big purple shards (hidden in tricky spots) are worth 5. Spend them on creepy new heads in the Heads Shop.
  • Every 10 levels the look changes, new monsters show up, and there's a boss on Level 10, 20, 30... You unlock a new attack every 20 levels to help with the next boss. The first is the Wisp Bolt after Level 20.
