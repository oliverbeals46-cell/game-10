HallowHeads

A tiny vessel. A cursed patch. A head for every haunting.

How to Play

  • Explore the Rotten Patch. Run, jump, wall-jump and dash your way through spooky ruins.
  • Fight grave mites and vesper bats with your slash. Hit them from above with a down-slash to bounce (it works on spikes too!).
  • Soul fills the orb in the corner every time you hit a foe. Spend it to heal one mask.
  • Candy is the currency. Collect it and spend it in the Heads Shop on new Halloween heads — each one has its own perk.
  • Lanterns are checkpoints. Light one to save your spot and refill your health.
  • Beat the boss at the end of each level to unlock a new attack. Level 1's boss, the Gourd King, guards the Wisp Bolt.
