HallowHeads

A hollow wanderer. A cursed graveyard. Something is always watching.

How to Play

  • It's an obby! Run, jump, double jump, wall-jump and dash through an obstacle course all the way to the glowing finish portal.
  • No health bar. Touch spikes, lava or fire and you die, then pop right back at your last checkpoint. Your deaths are counted in the top-right.
  • Checkpoints are numbered flags. Touch one and it turns green. That's where you come back after you die.
  • Beat your time. Your time for the level and your best time are shown in the top-right corner.
  • Mini monsters roam the course. They only knock you back. Jump over them, or fight them to earn souls. Down-slash them (or spikes) to bounce!
  • Signs (the ? posts) explain each obstacle and attack. Walk up to one to read it.
  • Souls are earned by defeating monsters and finishing levels (bonus souls for a new best time or no deaths). Spend them on creepy new heads in the Heads Shop. Each head has its own perk.
  • Every 10 levels the look changes, new monsters show up, and there's a boss on Level 10, 20, 30... You unlock a new attack every 20 levels to help with the next boss. The first is the Wisp Bolt after Level 20.
